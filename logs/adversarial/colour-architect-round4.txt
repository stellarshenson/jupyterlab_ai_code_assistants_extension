## (b) Defects

- **[MAJOR] The legacy-file rule is built on a false premise, so an upgrade re-opens the un-inheriting defect and cements it on the first write.** `colour_store.py:42-44` claims "the only writer that predates this was the tab write-back". It is not: `inherit_colour` was added in `8e62249`, which precedes `3ce0ced` ("post-publish 0.6.17 package metadata") - the published 0.6.x wrote fork-inherited entries into `state/<provider>-colours.json` with no `overrides` key. `colour_store.py:57-59` therefore returns `set(colours)` - every legacy entry, inherited ones included, classified hand-set. Reproduced against the venv with a 0.6.x-shaped file `{"colours": {"parent": "sky", "childA": "sky", "childB": "sky"}}`: `load_overrides("kimi") → ['childA', 'childB', 'parent']`, and one subsequent `set_colour` rewrites the file with `"overrides": ["childA","childB","other","parent"]` - the misclassification is now permanent, surviving any later fix. Failure scenario, Kimi (`derived`): user on 0.6.17 hand-set red on A and branched twice; upgrades; right-clicks the row and takes `Reset Tab Colours (3)`; the two branches lose their inherited tint and scatter to `fnv1aColour(ownId)`, breaking acc-crit "Branch colour inheritance" - the identical outcome my round-3 MAJOR described, through the item the user was invited to click. Fix, one line: `colour_store.py:59` → `return colours, set()`, mirrored at `colour.ts:178` → `(data.overrides ?? [])`, with `IColourStoreResponse.overrides` made required at `types.ts:274` so the fallback disappears entirely. Cost of that fix, stated plainly: a colour hand-set before the upgrade stops being releasable through the item, and re-picking the SAME colour on the tab will not re-capture it (see b5) - the user must pick a different colour first. That is a recoverable annoyance; the un-inheriting is silent, irreversible loss. Three artefacts assert the wrong rule and must flip with it: `colour_store.py:42-44`, `test_colour_store.py:295` (`test_a_file_written_before_origins_treats_its_colours_as_hand_set`), `colour.spec.ts:226`, and `docs/acc-crit-…:270` ("A store written before origins were recorded treats its colours as hand-set").
- **[MINOR] Five call sites of `reconcileColours`, four rejection disciplines - and the round-3 fix is what proved the promise can reject.** `terminals.ts:296-364` has `try`/`finally` with no `catch`, and `_applyColour` → `this._colourfulTabs.setColour` (`:390`) calls into another extension that can throw. Sites: `terminals.ts:257` `void`, `:274` `void`, `panel.ts:544` `void load().then(reconcile)` unguarded, `panel.ts:943` bare `await` inside `_resetColours` (itself `void`-ed at `:1757` with no catch), `panel.ts:1983-1984` the only `.catch`.
