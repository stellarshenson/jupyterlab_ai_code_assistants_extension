The tab ends bare and the choice is gone, where the pre-fix-7 code lost nothing. Reproduced both directions in `/tmp/.../scratchpad/probe/` against copies of `colour.ts`: mid-flight `get(A)==='mint'` **and** `isOverride(A)===true` (both reads the gate makes), then both revert on the failed write; with the `_inFlight` block at `colour.ts:187-199` removed, the same interleaving yields `get(A)===null` / `isOverride(A)===false`, i.e. `held === false` and no paint. **Fix, four lines and no new layer**: a `isPending(id)` predicate on `ColourStore` (`return !!id && this._inFlight.has(id)`) and `&& !this._colours.isPending(observedId)` on the `held` expression at `terminals.ts:329-332`.
